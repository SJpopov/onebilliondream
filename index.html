The story

When I was a child,
I had big dreams.

I wanted to be an explorer — to do things no one had done before. I wanted to travel to space. I wanted to make discoveries that would matter. I wanted to live a life that felt genuinely extraordinary.

Then life happened. And at some point I looked around and realized: I might not get there on my own.

So instead of pretending otherwise, I decided to do something different. I decided to be completely, almost embarrassingly honest:

I need help. I want to be financially free — not for the money itself, but for what it unlocks. Free to dream, to explore, to achieve every single thing I've ever wanted.

No charity cause. No product. No promise of anything in return. Just one person asking honestly for help. The first milestone is simple: if one million people choose to give one dollar, the dream gets its first real chance.

  • Travel to space before I die
  • Fund an expedition to somewhere unexplored
  • Never worry about money again
  • Wake up each morning and ask "what shall I discover today?"
  • Prove that asking is braver than not asking

Is this naive? Absolutely. Is it worth trying? I believe so.
